# Stakeholder Analysis Summary

## Priority Stakeholders

The two stakeholders requiring primary project attention due to their High Influence and High Interest ratings are **S01 (Head of the IT Department / Sponsor)** and **S02 (Supervising Lecturer / Project Manager)**. The sponsor holds ultimate funding authority and evaluates project success against departmental goals, making their continued alignment critical at every phase gate. The Project Manager oversees day-to-day execution, technical alignment, and risk management, serving as the core director of team activities.

## Veto Stakeholder

**S08 (Data Protection Officer)** serves as the critical "veto" stakeholder for the Realtime Quiz project. Although their day-to-day interest level is low (L), they hold High influence (H) regarding legal and governance compliance. If student identity or quiz performance data fails privacy standards, S08 possesses the formal authority to halt the live pilot in Week 14.
